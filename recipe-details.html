Chicken Biryani

A flavorful and aromatic rice dish made with tender chicken, fragrant spices and basmati rice.

⏱️ 45 min Cooking Time
👨‍🍳 Medium Difficulty
🍽️ 4 Servings

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 500g chicken
  • 2 onions
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 2 tsp chili powder
  • Biryani masala
  • Fresh coriander and mint
  • Salt according to taste
  • Cooking oil

Cooking Steps

01

Prepare the Chicken

Mix chicken with yogurt, spices, ginger garlic paste and salt. Let it marinate for 20 minutes.

02

Cook the Rice

Boil basmati rice with salt and whole spices until it is about 70% cooked.

03

Cook the Chicken

Heat oil and cook onions until golden. Add marinated chicken and cook well.

04

Layer the Biryani

Add rice over the chicken. Add mint and coriander. Cover and cook on low flame.

05

Serve

Mix gently and serve hot with raita or your favorite side dish.
