Chicken Biryani

A delicious aromatic rice dish layered with juicy chicken, herbs and traditional spices.

⏱️ 45 min
👨‍🍳 Medium
🍽️ 4 Servings
🛒 WHAT YOU NEED

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 500g chicken
  • 2 onions
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • Ginger garlic paste
  • 2 tablespoons biryani masala
  • Fresh coriander
  • Fresh mint
  • Salt to taste
  • Cooking oil
👩‍🍳 LET'S COOK

Cooking Steps

01

Prepare the Rice

Wash the basmati rice and cook it until it is about 70% done. Drain and keep aside.

02

Prepare the Chicken

Marinate chicken with yogurt, spices, ginger garlic paste and salt.

03

Cook the Masala

Fry onions until golden. Add tomatoes, spices and marinated chicken.

04

Layer the Biryani

Add cooked rice over the chicken. Top with mint, coriander and fried onions.

05

Cook and Serve

Cover and cook on low heat for 15 minutes. Serve hot and enjoy!

💡 CHEF'S TIP

Let the flavors come together.

Give the biryani a few minutes to rest before serving. This helps the flavors blend beautifully.
